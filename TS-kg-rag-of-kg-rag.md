## §1 · The 6 rules

Everything else in this document answers to one of these. Four were written as doctrine cards; `authority` and `provenance` were stated repeatedly in contracts and code without ever being named as rules, and 13 findings answered to nothing until they were.


**absence-is-not-a-result** — A check whose failure mode is indistinguishable from its success mode (silence means no error) reports nothing rather than passing. Every check must assert something positive — a count — not merely the lack of an exception. [[node:doctrine_rule::absence-is-not-a-result]]


**authority** — Knowing what something is does not confer permission to act on it, and issuing a directive is not performing it. Grounding, authority and effect are separate declarations. A receipt makes an assertion verifiable, not true; retrieved content is not trusted content; and coverage must be asserted per call-site, never per codebase. [[node:doctrine_rule::authority]]


**declaration** — Retrieval improves only by adding information the system lacked, not by rearranging what it already has. A declared dimension must distinguish within a query's candidate set, or it is a near-constant that displaces signal and lowers recall. [[node:doctrine_rule::declaration]]


**measurement** — A comparative claim needs a statistical gate or it is NOT_ESTABLISHED, no matter how convincing the story. Sample size is set by discordant flips, not raw n. No model may sit in the decision path. [[node:doctrine_rule::measurement]]


**provenance** — A copy of a fact has no expiry date. Every derived value is either traceable to its input or an explicit refusal, a human declaration is never clobbered by a re-derivation, and a pointer carrying the digest of what it points at cannot drift undetected. [[node:doctrine_rule::provenance]]


**retrieval** — Find the stage that actually decides an outcome before fixing anything — ablate, do not guess from the symptom. New content is not free: gate additions on the existing gold set's regressions. [[node:doctrine_rule::retrieval]]
